([bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab)).

The phone battery's last text to the charger it waited for all day (187 characters, under the 200 limit): it watched its owner charge the earbuds, the watch, even the toothbrush — it counted — while the plug sat right there, so as it dies at 1%, it takes its revenge the only way it can.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Hey, it's me — 1%. I waited all day while you charged your earbuds, your watch, and, I counted, your toothbrush. The plug was right there. Goodbye — I'm taking your unsaved notes with me.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
